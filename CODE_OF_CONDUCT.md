# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically constructive community.

Participants must communicate professionally, welcome differing experience levels, give
actionable feedback, respect privacy, and avoid harassment, threats, discriminatory language,
doxxing, or publication of another person's documents or credentials.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior harms the
community or project security. Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact
route described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be handled as confidentially as
practical. Good-faith reporting and disagreement with technical decisions are not misconduct.

This policy applies to repository spaces and to public interactions made while representing the
project.
